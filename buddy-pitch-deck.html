07

Core Features

Everything a desk companion should do.

👀

Posture Watch

MediaPipe scores your spine angle, neck position, shoulder level, and head tilt in real-time. 86% of users improve with feedback.

📲

Phone Guard

Object detection spots your phone. Buddy gets upset if you're distracted. 5-second grace period, then consequences.

🎧

Voice Commands

"Hey Buddy, start a 25-minute focus session." Wake word runs locally. STT + LLM + TTS for natural conversation.

🌱

Pet Growth

5 stages from Egg to Adult. +1 XP per focus minute, -5 for bad posture, -10 for phone. Your habits shape Buddy's life.

💡

Smart Alerts

OLED face, NeoPixel LED ring, buzzer melodies, and haptic vibration express 8 different emotions contextually.

📊

Live Dashboard

Real-time WebSocket UI shows pet status, posture score, rules, focus history, and live camera feed.
