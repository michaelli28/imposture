6. Imagery & Icons Plan
Pet Character
Pixel art sprites rendered on the warm cream background. The same pixel art style used on the OLED display, adapted for the web with larger resolution. Shows emotions: happy, sad, angry, sleeping, excited.
Icons
Lucide Icons — rounded, friendly line icons that match the warm aesthetic. Consistent 1.5px stroke width. Orange primary, slate for secondary.
Decorative Elements
Soft floating blobs in the hero section (peach/orange gradients). Subtle dot grid pattern on the background. Rounded corners everywhere (14-24px).
